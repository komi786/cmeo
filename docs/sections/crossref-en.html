comparator specificationc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CMEO_0000071

It refers to a benchmark or reference against which the effects of a medical intervention are compared. In clinical trials, the comparator can be a placebo (an inactive substance designed to resemble the drug being tested), a different dose or regimen of the drug being studied, or a different drug altogether. The choice of comparator is a critical aspect of clinical trial design and can significantly impact the interpretation and applicability of the trial's results.
